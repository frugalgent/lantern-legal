1. Who we are

The App is provided by [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY], located at [ADDRESS, if required]. For privacy questions, contact us at [privacy@yourdomain.com].

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a unique identifier from your sign-in provider (email, Sign in with Apple, or Google). You may optionally add a display name.
  • Content you create. Your journal entries, reading-plan progress, and reading streaks. Journal entries are personal reflections and may reveal your religious beliefs — we treat them as sensitive data (see Section 5).
  • AI Study questions. When you use the AI Study companion, the questions you type and the Bible passage you are viewing are sent to our service provider to generate a response (see Section 4).

Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics. We collect product-analytics events (for example, that the app was opened, a plan was started, or a journal entry was created) and coarse metadata, to understand engagement and improve the App. We do not send the text of your journal entries, your Scripture reading, or your Study messages to our analytics provider.
  • Diagnostics / crash data. If the App crashes or errors, we collect diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app version, error details) to fix problems. We disable the collection of personal identifiers in crash reports where possible.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have an active "Lantern Plus" subscription, and related transaction metadata, via our subscriptions provider. We do not receive your full payment-card details — those are handled by Apple or Google.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The Bible text itself is bundled in the App and read on your device offline; your everyday reading does not require a network connection or get sent to us.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide and operate the App and your account;
  • Save and sync your journal, plans, and streaks across your sessions;
  • Provide the AI Study companion;
  • Process and manage your subscription;
  • Send local reminder notifications you have enabled (daily verse, streak reminder) — these are scheduled on your device;
  • Monitor, debug, secure, and improve the App;
  • Communicate with you about the App and respond to support requests;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. The AI Study companion

When you ask the AI Study companion a question, the App sends your question, the short recent conversation, and the text of the passage you are studying to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), through our backend, which returns a response. We use this only to generate your answer.

  • We store a daily count of how many AI messages you have sent (to enforce usage limits), but we do not store the content of your Study conversations on our servers.
  • Per Anthropic's API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train their models.

The AI companion can be wrong or incomplete and is not a substitute for professional, medical, legal, or pastoral advice.

5. Sensitive personal data (your journal & beliefs)

Your journal entries, and your use of a Bible study app generally, may reveal information about your religious or philosophical beliefs, which is considered sensitive personal data under laws such as the GDPR.

  • We collect and process this information only to provide the App's features to you (storing and showing you your own reflections).
  • Where required by law, our legal basis is your explicit consent, which you provide by choosing to create journal entries; you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your entries or your account.
  • Journal content is protected by access controls so that only your account can read it, is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider, and is not used for analytics, advertising, or model training.
